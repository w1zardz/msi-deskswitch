- Logitech HID++ HiRes Wheel specification: https://lekensteyn.nl/files/logitech/x2121_hires_wheel.pdf
- Solaar's feature definitions: https://github.com/pwr-Solaar/Solaar
- Windows HID and monitor APIs: https://learn.microsoft.com/windows-hardware/drivers/hid/ and https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/win32/monitor/monitor-configuration
